How we check a deal

Every price on this page was read at the named store, or in a deals source that names the store, on 27 November 2026. The discount is worked out from the two prices shown, so the numbers always add up. NEW LOW means the lowest price seen for that listing; STRONG DEAL is a large genuine cut; ROUTINE CUT is real but small or seen before. Prices move fast on Black Friday — check the price at the store before you buy. Store links open the store's own search for the product (the PlayStation VR2 link is Sony's product page).

Deal Scout is an independent roundup, not a store. Product names and store names belong to their owners. No prices here are sponsored.
